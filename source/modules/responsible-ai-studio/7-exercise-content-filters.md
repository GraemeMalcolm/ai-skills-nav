One of the most effective ways to mitigate harmful responses from generative AI models in Microsoft Foundry is to use *guardrail controls*. In this exercise, you deploy an AI model and observe the effect of guardrails on the responses it returns.

> **NOTE**: To complete this lab, you need an [Azure subscription](https://azure.microsoft.com/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account?cid=msft_learn).
