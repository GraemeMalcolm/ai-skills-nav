If you're already using Microsoft Copilot Chat to answer questions or draft content, you've seen what it can do at a basic level. In this module, you'll take it further. You'll learn how to ground Copilot in your work data, use grounding modes to get more relevant results, and work with agents to handle more complex tasks. By the end, you'll be able to use Copilot Chat more intentionally to support your day-to-day work.

## What you can do with Copilot Chat

Copilot Chat works with you across Microsoft 365 apps like Teams, Outlook, and the Microsoft Copilot app. You can go beyond simple question-and-answer to support real work, including:

- Get contextual answers - Ground Copilot in your emails, files, meetings, and chats to get responses that reflect your work context.
- Create and refine content - Draft documents, generate images, and turn responses into shared, editable Copilot Pages.
- Analyze files - Upload documents to summarize, extract key points, or explore content in more detail.
- Work with agents - Use specialized assistants to help with research, analysis, and multi-step tasks.

## Agents in Copilot Chat

Agents help you go beyond a single prompt. Instead of trying to write a complex request to gather information from multiple sources, you can use an agent designed for that task. Agents can gather information, run multi-step research, and in some cases take action on your behalf. Copilot Chat includes access to several types of agents, depending on your license:

- Pay-as-you-go agents Priced per use; can access organizational content through a metered model
- Prompt Coach Guides you through building more effective prompts through interactive conversation
- Writing Coach Reviews your writing and suggests improvements for clarity, tone, and overall impact
- Idea Coach Facilitates brainstorming and helps you develop, organize, and refine creative ideas
- Researcher Handles complex, multi-step research tasks using both web and organizational data (premium)
- Analyst Performs advanced data analysis, visualizations, and calculations with no coding required (premium)
- Facilitator Captures real-time notes and action items during Teams meetings (premium)
- Custom agents Built by your organization to connect Copilot to specific data sources and business processes

Agents become more effective when they can access your work context, including your files, emails, meetings, and preferences.

In this module, you'll use Prompt Coach to craft more effective prompts and get stronger responses. You'll see how the Researcher agent can handle more complex questions by pulling together information from multiple sources.
