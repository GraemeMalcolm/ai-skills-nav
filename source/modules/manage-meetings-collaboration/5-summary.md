In this module, you explored how Microsoft Copilot supports your entire meeting workflow—from preparation through follow-up and ongoing collaboration. Copilot in Outlook and Teams helps you prepare, stay engaged, and capture what matters. Copilot Chat keeps stakeholders informed with timely updates. Copilot Notebooks gives your team a centralized workspace for analysis, research, and decision-making. With a Microsoft Copilot license, Pages extends that work into shared, work-grounded project hubs your whole team can build on together.

You also completed an exercise where you applied Copilot across Outlook, Teams, and Copilot Pages in a realistic project scenario.

Now that you've completed this module, you're able to:

- Use Copilot in Outlook and Teams to prepare for meetings, capture real-time insights, and generate post-meeting summaries and action items.
- Use the Facilitator agent to automate collaborative note taking and keep meetings focused and on track.
- Draft effective follow-up communications and keep your team aligned using Copilot Chat.
- Organize project content and generate insights using Copilot Notebooks, and build shared project hubs using Copilot Pages.
