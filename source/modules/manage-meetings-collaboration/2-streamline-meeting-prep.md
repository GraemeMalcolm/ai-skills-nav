You're leading the LaunchPad project check-in at Contoso. Before the meeting, you need a calendar invite ready; during the meeting, you want to capture what's said in real time without stopping to take notes; and after, you need a clear summary and a follow-up ready to send. Copilot in Outlook and Teams is built for exactly this workflow.

## Schedule a meeting with Copilot in Outlook

Copilot in Outlook can create a calendar invite for you directly. Select the Copilot icon to open the Copilot pane, then switch the mode from **Default mode** to **Allow actions** so Copilot can take direct actions on your calendar — like creating the event itself, not just drafting text. Describe what you need and Copilot proposes the full invite including a title, attendees, and agenda. Review the details and select **Approve** to add it to your calendar.

- **Draft a Teams meeting invite for a 15-minute LaunchPad project check-in today at [time]. Include an agenda covering progress updates, open blockers, and next steps.**

> **NOTE**: Learn more about [Copilot in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/topic/chat-with-copilot-in-outlook-8090e7b3-5b1d-4c6d-9b06-02edac062f58).

## Prepare for a meeting with Copilot Chat

Before a high-stakes meeting, Copilot can pull together everything relevant from your emails, files, and meetings in one prompt — saving you from sifting through dozens of messages manually.

> **NOTE**: You can run preparation prompts from the Copilot pane in Outlook or from Copilot Chat — both have access to your emails, files, and meetings.

- **Summarize this email thread about the launch timeline. Then create an agenda to discuss scheduling and action items.**
- **Summarize everything related to the LaunchPad project from my emails, files, and meetings over the past two weeks so I'm fully prepared for today's review.**

## During the meeting with Copilot in Teams

Copilot in Teams gives you an AI assistant that works alongside you during meetings — capturing what's said, answering questions in real time, and helping the group stay on track, all without breaking the conversation.

During a meeting, Copilot can:

- Summarize discussions in real time, including who said what and where people agree or disagree.
- Suggest action items, follow-ups, and questions to move the conversation forward.
- Answer questions about the meeting using transcript and chat data.
- Create tables, lists, and summaries based on meeting content.

### How Copilot works in Teams meetings

To use Copilot during a meeting, start **recording** first — this generates the transcript Copilot needs to summarize, answer questions, and track topics. If recording isn't available in your organization, use **Transcribe** instead. Without a transcript, Copilot is limited to chat-based data only.

> **NOTE**: Copilot requires a transcript to provide full meeting insights. Without one, its functionality is limited to chat-based data.

Once recording is running, select the **Copilot** icon in the meeting toolbar to open the Copilot Chat pane. You can interact with it throughout the meeting without interrupting the conversation.

### Prompts to use during the meeting

- **What are the key takeaways so far?**
- **Where do we disagree?**
- **Summarize what each participant said.**
- **Create a table of pros and cons for each idea.**
- **Tell me what I missed so far.** Use this prompt if you join the meeting late.

> **TIP**: Mention agents like Researcher or Analyst in your prompts using the `@` symbol to dive more deeply into data or insights. For example: **@Analyst, summarize the sales data mentioned so far and highlight any trends.**

### Facilitator agent

Alongside Copilot in Teams, the **Facilitator** agent acts like a team member inside your meeting. Unlike Copilot—where your interactions are private to you—Facilitator is shared: when anyone types **@Facilitator** in the meeting chat, all participants see the interaction.

Facilitator automatically takes collaborative notes, tracks topics with visual timeline markers, and helps the group stay focused. After the meeting, it can generate a Word document summarizing notes and action items—useful for sharing decisions across your team.

> **NOTE**: Facilitator must be enabled by an IT administrator.

## After the meeting with Copilot in Teams

After a meeting ends, open the **Recap** tab in the meeting chat and select **AI summary** to see a structured summary of what was discussed, who contributed, where the team aligned or disagreed, and what follow-up tasks were proposed. This summary is built from the transcript, so it's grounded in what was actually said — not paraphrased from memory.

This capability — known as **Intelligent recap** — is available for scheduled, instant, and ad-hoc meetings when a transcript exists. You can browse the recording by speaker and topic, and access AI-generated notes, tasks, and @mentions all in one place. Summaries include citations for transparency and can be exported to Word or Excel for further collaboration.

To draft a follow-up from the recap, use Copilot Chat and reference the meeting directly using `/` followed by the meeting name — this grounds Copilot's response in the specific meeting's content rather than general knowledge:

- **Draft a follow-up email for the LaunchPad Team summarizing /LaunchPad Project Check-In. Include the open blockers and list follow-up tasks with owners and due dates.**
- **List action items from the meeting transcript for each team member.**

> **NOTE**: Explore more prompts for meeting summaries in the [Copilot Prompt Lab](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/copilot-prompts).
