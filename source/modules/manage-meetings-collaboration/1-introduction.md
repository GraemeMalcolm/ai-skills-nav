Imagine you're part of the LaunchPad Team at Contoso — a cross-functional group working together to launch a new productivity app. Like many business users, you and your teammates juggle multiple meetings, emails, and tasks daily. Preparing for the next meeting means sifting through email threads and documents, and after a meeting you still need to capture decisions, assign actions, and keep the team aligned.

Microsoft Copilot steps in as your AI assistant — helping you schedule meetings, capture real-time notes, draft follow-up communications, and centralize project content so you can focus on the work that matters. The module wraps up with an exercise where you can apply these tools across a full meeting workflow.

By the end of this module, you're able to:

- Use Copilot in Outlook and Teams to prepare for meetings, capture real-time insights, and generate post-meeting summaries and action items.
- Use the Facilitator agent to automate collaborative note taking and keep meetings focused and on track.
- Draft effective follow-up communications and keep your team aligned using Copilot Chat.
- Organize project content and generate insights using Copilot Notebooks, and build shared project hubs using Copilot Pages.
